Particle Life Simulation Guide

What is Particle Life?

Particle Life is a continuous multi-agent artificial life simulator. When simple attractive and repulsive forces operate between different types (species) of particles, complex emergent behaviors spontaneously arise—including self-propelling organisms (worms), dividing cellular membranes, orbits, and crystal lattices.

How the Interaction Matrix Works

The 4x4 matrix defines the force exerted between particle species A (Coral), B (Emerald), C (Sky), and D (Lavender).

  • Positive values (+0.1 to +1.0): Attraction. The source species pursues or clings to the target species.
  • Negative values (-0.1 to -1.0): Repulsion. The source species flees from the target species.
  • Asymmetric pairs (e.g. A attracts B, but B repels A): Produces perpetual kinetic chase loops, forming swimming snakes and creatures!

Interactive Mouse Controls

  • Left Click & Drag: Triggers active tool (Attractor Gravity Well, Repulsor Field, Vortex Swirl, or Particle Emitter).
  • Spacebar: Pause / Resume simulation.
  • Period (.): Step a single frame while paused.
